◉ Glance

More than half of the web's most popular home pages have images with no text description (WebAIM Million). On those images, screen reader users hear image or a file name. Glance writes the missing descriptions with Gemini Nano on your device. It uses cloud Gemini only when a page is public and the image is too complex for Nano.

    How Glance routes each image

    1. Privacy first. Mail, banking, health and personal-photo sites, pages with a password field, and sites you mark private use ONLY_ON_DEVICE. The image never leaves your computer.
    2. Quick pass on Gemini Nano. It writes the alt text and classifies the image (photo, chart, screenshot…) with a confidence score.
    3. Escalate only when it's worth it. Charts, screenshots and images with text on public pages get a detailed read from cloud Gemini (ONLY_IN_CLOUD).
    4. Shared cache. Descriptions of public images are stored in Firestore, so the next person gets them instantly at zero cost. Private images are never stored.
    5. Tuned live. Sensitive-site rules, escalation thresholds and models come from Firebase Remote Config. Requests are protected by App Check.
